# apps/assistant/core/tools/team/

`message_teammate`: the assistant's only road to start a Signal conversation with a person in the registry.

- Refused unless `ASSISTANT_TEAM_MESSAGE` is on, and refused at dispatch on the patrol surface.
- A word cap, a language check against the person's profile, and a refusal to repeat an item already told and unmoved. These bind what the door sends; the gateway's own replies cross none of them.
