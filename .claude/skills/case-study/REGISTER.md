# The case-study register

The voice a drawn page and its note are written in, and what the set actually holds today. The refute step's voice refuter reads this file.

## The page

- **Plain words, house voice.** Everyday language, English only, no marketing voice, no em dashes, no jargon the glossary (`CONTEXT.md`) does not carry. Name a box by the plain name a person would say out loud.
- **The subtitle is one honest line.** What the page is about, including the awkward part, never a slogan.
- **Notes carry the argument, not an inventory.** Two to four sentences per box: why it is there, the one load-bearing fact, what will bite a reader. Facts good, unneeded detail out.
- **A fact belongs to one page.** A box that repeats what another page owns loses the fact; the page that owns it keeps it.
- **Captions never repeat the arrow.** An edge label says what moves or why it matters, or it is empty.
- **The shape is the argument.** Each page takes the shape its own content has, told one slide at a time, opening on a single box or one tight region, six boxes at most. Anything wider collapses into groups that open their own page: depth is another page, not a denser drawing. The rules are [`docs/diagrams/tools/REDRAW-BRIEF.md`](../../../docs/diagrams/tools/REDRAW-BRIEF.md).
- **Complete lives in the levels.** The top page keeps what carries the story and gathers the plumbing into named groups; nothing real is left out of the set, the switched-off pieces included and plainly marked off.
- **Never ship unverified claims.** A number on a page traces to the code, a measurement or an incident.
- **A human clicks through before it is called done.** Adversarial agents check facts; a person finds the wrong route, the dead link, the claim that overreaches. Fix on the spot.

## The note

Only for the rare piece that has to stand on its own outside the repo.

- **Neutral case study, never first person.** The subjects are the method, the engineer, the assistant, the repository, the team. Quoted speech may keep its person.
- **The artifacts lead.** The diagrams are the core; the note carries only the why, the proof and the caveats. No node-level or mechanism commentary in the text, the popups carry that level. Weave the page links into the prose exactly where it touches their content.
- **Story first, references as support.** A paragraph opens with the account; citations are trailing clauses, never paragraph subjects. Only web-verified references, and few.
- **Introduce every subject before its numbers.** Written for a reader outside the project; gloss the first use of any internal name.
- **Honesty caveats travel inline.** A figure that mixes signals says so in the same sentence; timing claims are correlation unless controlled; automated shares and proxies are named.
- **Plain and flowing.** Short sentences with real transitions, no stacked colon-and-semicolon chains, no diminutives, English only.
